## 1. Specific Aims (6-Month Phase I Performance Period)

Per the official Grants.gov forecast for **`HHS-2027-ACL-NIDILRR-BISA-0308`**, ACL/NIDILRR SBIR Phase I grants (*6-month project period, up to \$100,000*) support R&D products generated by small businesses that **improve the lives of people with disabilities and promote their health and function, community living, or employment outcomes**. During disasters, power outages, and housing disruptions, people with mobility, sensory, cognitive, or power-dependent medical disabilities face extreme risk because general shelter directories lack verified accessibility telemetry (wheelchair/ADA ramp access, backup power for durable medical equipment [DME], accessible restrooms, sensory-quiet spaces, ASL/communication support, and service-animal accommodation).

**Digital Nomad LLC** proposes a 6-month Phase I R&D program building upon our existing **TRL-6 `Shelter Source` & `Nomad Nexus` Flutter/Firebase platform** (`/home/psycry/Projects/shelter_source`) to establish technical merit and feasibility across three Specific Aims:

* **Specific Aim 1: Accessible Geospatial Schema & WCAG 2.1 AA Cross-Platform Navigation Core (`TRL-6 -> TRL-7`)**
  * Extend `Shelter Source`'s unified iOS/Android/Web Flutter mapping core with a standardized **NIDILRR Disability & Accessibility Resource Schema** covering ADA physical access, DME backup generator capacity, sensory/cognitive accommodations, accessible transit proximity, and independent-living support centers, paired with a screen-reader-optimized (TalkBack/VoiceOver), high-contrast, low-cognitive-load UI.
  * *Quantitative KPI:* Map rendering and accessibility-filter query latency $< 2.0\text{s}$ across 100+ regional nodes; 100% pass rate on automated WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility audits across iOS, Android, and Web targets.

* **Specific Aim 2: Offline-First Resilience for Power/Connectivity Outages (`TRL-6 -> TRL-7`)**
  * Engineer deterministic local persistence (SQLite/Hive) and conflict-free background synchronization so individuals with disabilities, caregivers, and independent-living coordinators retain full search, routing, and accessibility attribute filtering during cellular blackouts.
  * *Quantitative KPI:* $\ge 95\%$ synchronization integrity across simulated intermittent/offline transitions; zero data loss of critical accessibility status flags.

* **Specific Aim 3: Community & Center-for-Independent-Living (CIL) Verification Pipeline (`TRL-6 -> TRL-7`)**
  * Prototype a multi-tiered crowdsourcing and verification workflow enabling people with disabilities, CIL staff, and municipal emergency managers to report real-time accessibility conditions (e.g., elevator outage, generator fuel status, accessible cot capacity) backed by Firestore security rules and reputation-weighted moderation.
  * *Quantitative KPI:* $\ge 80\%$ automated verification pipeline throughput under simulated 1,000-user load; Phase I usability evaluation with $\ge 15$ disability community stakeholders/operators achieving $\ge 4.0/5.0$ System Usability & Accessibility Satisfaction.

## 2. Significance, Innovation & Existing TRL-6 Prior Art

### Significance & Alignment with NIDILRR Outcome Domains
1. **Community Living & Participation:** Enables people with disabilities to navigate daily accessible civic resources (accessible housing navigation, food assistance, transit, and Centers for Independent Living) and evacuate safely to verified accessible shelters during emergencies without institutionalization.
2. **Health & Function:** Protects individuals reliant on electricity-dependent durable medical equipment (ventilators, oxygen concentrators, power wheelchairs, refrigerated medications) by surfacing real-time shelter backup-power and medical-support telemetry.

### Existing TRL-6 Prior Art (`Shelter Source` & `Nomad Nexus`)
Digital Nomad LLC brings an operational **TRL-6** codebase at `/home/psycry/Projects/shelter_source` and `/home/psycry/Projects/nomad_nexus`:
- **Unified Flutter/Dart Cross-Platform Architecture:** Single codebase targeting iOS, Android, and Web with real-time geospatial indexing.
- **Firebase Auth & Cloud Firestore Security Rules:** Role-based access control and composite geospatial/attribute indexing for community-contributed resource updates.
- **Offline-First Persistence Modules:** Local caching and state synchronization proven across low-connectivity field environments.
